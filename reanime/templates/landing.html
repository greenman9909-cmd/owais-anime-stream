Built like a product

Fast where it matters.

The interface keeps the heavy lifting out of the critical path: local history, lean catalogue requests, a compact Android shell and provider logic isolated behind a single adapter.

Playback architecture

Safety defaults

Clean by default.

The catalogue query excludes adult titles, and no ad or watermark bypass code is bundled. The app adds its own chrome around provider content without pretending provider video is first-party.
